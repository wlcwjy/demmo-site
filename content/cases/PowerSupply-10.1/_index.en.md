## Introduction

----

<div style="text-align: justify;">

&emsp;&emsp;The Smart Pulse Power Supply is a portable smart power device specifically designed for coil driving. Based on a high-performance MCU core, the system integrates a boost drive circuit and precision pulse generation logic. It supports three adjustable levels of high-frequency, high-voltage pulse output, providing stable excitation energy for various coil loads through precise duty cycle and frequency control. Featuring a minimalist single-button and digital tube interaction design, along with a comprehensive battery management and safety protection system, it is ideal for driving and controlling electromagnets, micro vibrators, and various induction coils.
</div>

## Functional Specifications

### 1. Core Electrical Specifications

------

<div style="text-align: justify;">

> **Pulse Output Mode:**
>  - **Output Voltage**: **24V** (Generated by the internal boost circuit).
>  - **Duty Cycle**: Fixed at **20%**.
>  - **Current Characteristics**: Average current of **0.1A**, with a momentary peak current of **0.5A**.

> **Frequency Levels:**
>   - **Level 1**: **10Hz** (Low-frequency, high-force drive).
>   - **Level 2**: **40Hz** (Mid-frequency, balanced drive).
>   - **Level 3**: **80Hz** (High-frequency, fast-response drive).

> **Power Management Parameters:**
>  - **Cell Discharge Cutoff Voltage**: Single cell **2.9V** (Precise detection to prevent over-discharge).
>  - **Capacity Release Target**: Ensures that **90%** of the total battery capacity is released before the cutoff threshold is reached.

> **Charging Input:**
>  - **Interface Type**: USB Type-C.
>  - **Input Voltage**: **5V DC**.

</div>

### 2. Hardware Architecture and Key Components

------

<div style="text-align: justify;">

> **Power Drive Stage:**
>  - **Boost Inductor and MOSFET**: A high-power shielded inductor combined with an N-MOSFET is onboard to build a BOOST step-up topology, boosting the battery voltage to 24V to drive the coil load.
>  - **Output Interface**: Wire soldering pads (Output Pads) are reserved for directly connecting the coil leads.

> **Main Control and Interaction Unit:**
>  - **MCU Controller**: The core processing unit responsible for PWM waveform generation, button scanning, and digital tube dynamic scanning.
>  - **Digital Tube Display**: An onboard LED digital tube is used to intuitively display the remaining battery percentage and the current working level.
>  - **Tactile Button**: Located at the edge of the board for power on/off, level switching, and start/stop control.

> **Power Interfaces:**
>  - **USB Type-C Receptacle**: Supports 5V charging input.
>  - **Battery Connection Terminal**: Connects to the lithium battery pack via soldering wires or terminal blocks.

</div>

### 3. Safety Protection System

------

<div style="text-align: justify;">

>To ensure the safety of both the coil drive and the equipment, the controller incorporates multiple hardware and software protection mechanisms:

> - **Over-Discharge Protection (UVLO)**: Real-time monitoring of the cell voltage; the discharge circuit is immediately cut off and pulse output is stopped once the **2.9V** cutoff threshold is reached.

> - **No-Load/Timeout Auto Power-Off**: If the system is in a state of no output and no button operation for more than **5 seconds**, it automatically enters power-off mode to prevent accidental battery depletion.

> - **Output Short-Circuit Protection**: If a short circuit occurs at the 24V output terminal, the hardware circuit shuts down the MOSFET within milliseconds to prevent damage to the drive chip.

> - **Charging Protection**: A built-in charge management chip prevents damage to the internal circuit from over-voltage or reverse connection during 5V input.


</div>

### 4. Human-Machine Interaction and Logic Control

------

<div style="text-align: justify;">

> **Button Operation Logic (Single Multi-function Button):**
> - **Power On / Level Switching:**
>    - 1st Short Press: Power on, default output at **Level 1 (10Hz)**.
>    - 2nd Short Press: Switch to **Level 2 (40Hz)**.
>    - 3rd Short Press: Switch to **Level 3 (80Hz)**.
>    - *Note: If the pulse is currently outputting, a short press cannot switch levels; the output must be stopped first.*

> **Start/Stop Output:**
>    - **Long Press**: Starts pulse output; if currently outputting, a long press will stop the output.
>    - **Auto Sleep**: The device automatically powers off after **5 seconds** of no output and no button operation.

> **Display Interaction:**
>    - **Alternating Display**: The screen cyclically alternates between displaying the **remaining battery percentage** and the **current level number** (1/2/3).

> **Timing Logic:**
>  - **Default Duration**: A single operation cycle is set to **30 minutes**.
>  - **Non-Cumulative Timing**: If stopped midway, restarting will initiate a new 30-minute cycle without accumulating the previous runtime.

</div>
